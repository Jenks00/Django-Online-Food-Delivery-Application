About us

A small kitchen, run like a real operation.

Basil started as a single neighbourhood kitchen with one simple idea: customers should always know exactly what is happening to their order, and the people cooking and delivering it should never be guessing either.

Every order that comes through the storefront lands directly on the kitchen's dashboard the moment it's placed. From there it moves through a clear pipeline — placed, cooking, ready for pickup — before handing off to the dispatch team, who take it out for delivery and mark it delivered the moment it reaches the door.

No phone calls back and forth, no paper tickets lost in the kitchen — just one shared view of every order from checkout to doorstep.
